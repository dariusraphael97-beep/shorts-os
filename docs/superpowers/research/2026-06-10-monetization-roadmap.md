**Compiled 2026-06-10. Current as of June 2026, verified against official YouTube Help/Blog sources.**
Goal: get **Dyfrx** (car niche, Shorts + longform) to **ad-revenue monetization (~by 2026-08)**.

## Key facts that shape the plan

1. **Two tiers, one goal.** The 500-sub tier unlocks *fan-funding only* — NOT ad revenue. Apply for it the moment eligible (free, gets the policy review done early), but the real target is the 1,000-sub + 4,000-hour full tier.
2. **Shorts views and longform hours are walled off.** Shorts watch time does **not** count toward the 4,000 longform hours. Shorts have their own 10M-views/90-day path. The one number both paths share is the 1,000 subscriber count.
3. **Longform is the faster + more lucrative monetization lever for us.** 4,000 hrs over a *forgiving 365-day window* beats 10M Shorts views in a *brutal rolling 90-day window* (a viral-tier number new channels rarely hit). Longform RPM ($1–$30) dwarfs Shorts RPM (~$0.01–$0.06). **The bottleneck is average view duration, not upload count** — exactly Dyfrx's current weak spot (~11% retention on the B58 video).
4. **The #1 demonetization risk is "inauthentic content" (renamed July 2025) — and it's about TEMPLATE SAMENESS, not AI.** AI is explicitly allowed. What gets rejected: mass-produced/templated videos with little variation and "no original insight." A rejection can taint the *entire channel*. Cure = every video carries an original take/thesis + structure/hook/visuals vary across videos. Our **factual-accuracy gate (operator ground-truth)** and per-niche angle ARE the originality signal. Source: [inauthentic-content policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en).
5. **AI disclosure: our illustrated style is largely exempt, and disclosure has zero reach/revenue penalty anyway.** The "altered/synthetic content" label is required only when a viewer could mistake AI output for *real footage of a real person/place/event*. Clearly-animated/illustrated content is exempt; script/idea/thumbnail assistance is exempt. Keep the narrator a synthetic (non-cloned) voice. Toggle disclosure ON for any photoreal-of-a-real-thing beat — it costs nothing. Sources: [YouTube blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/disclosing-ai-generated-content/) · [Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en).
6. **Hybrid Shorts + longform grows subscribers ~3× faster.** Shorts = subscriber/discovery engine (clears the 1,000-sub gate, seeds the longform audience); longform = the watch-hour bank. Always end a Short with a CTA/pin to the related longform.

## Fastest path for Dyfrx

**Target the longform path: 1,000 subs + 4,000 watch-hrs / 365 days. Shorts are the subscriber + discovery engine feeding it.** Do NOT chase 10M Shorts views — it's a trap for a new channel. The single highest-leverage KPI is **average view duration on longform** — fixing the slow hook compresses the timeline more than raw volume.

Baseline cadence the pipeline must sustain: **1 Short/day (7/wk) + 2 longform/wk.** Each Short pins/links the matching longform.

## 8-week plan

- **Week 1 — Foundation & hook fix.** 2-step verification ON. Apply for the 500-sub early tier when eligible. Ship 2 longform (8–12 min) + 7 Shorts. **Priority: fix the slow hook (B58 lesson)** — first 15 sec must hook → drives avg view duration, the #1 lever. Turn on AI-disclosure for any realistic-real-car beats.
- **Week 2 — Variation guardrails.** Same cadence. Enforce *material variation* — rotate intros, script arcs, thumbnails, on-screen hooks so nothing looks templated. Every video carries an original take/thesis. Start A/B-testing thumbnails + titles (B58 CTR was 2.9%; aim 5%+).
- **Week 3 — Double down.** Find the 1–2 best-performing topics/formats; make more *like them but materially different*. Shorts tease longform payoffs. Approach 1,000 subs.
- **Week 4 — Mid-point checkpoint.** Audit subs trajectory, cumulative longform hours, avg view duration trend. If avg view < ~3 min on 8-min videos, retention work overrides everything next week. Confirm fan-funding tier approved.
- **Week 5 — Length × retention.** If retention holds, lengthen top-format longform to 12–15 min (more hours/view) with tight pacing. Keep Shorts daily.
- **Week 6 — Watch-hour push.** Prioritize longform volume + binge-ability (playlists, end screens chaining videos). Maintain Shorts for sub top-up. Target ~2,000+ cumulative hours, 1,000 subs locked.
- **Week 7 — Close the gap.** Concentrate on the proven format. Community posts + Shorts CTAs drive existing audience to under-watched longform. Self-review the last ~20 uploads for templated outliers; unpublish/upgrade them before applying.
- **Week 8 — Apply & polish.** When 1,000 subs + 4,000 hrs/365 days are met and no active strikes: submit the full-tier YPP application. Keep cadence up during the ~weeks-long human review. Verify AI disclosures channel-wide.

## If we stall
- **Subs < 1,000:** Shorts problem → 2 Shorts/day, sharper first-frame hooks, double down on the best Short format.
- **Hours < 4,000:** Retention problem (most likely) → fix avg view duration; chapter, playlist, end-screen to chain views. *One 12-min video at 50% retention beats three 8-min at 11%.*
- **Rejected for "inauthentic/reused":** Highest-stakes. Cut the most templated videos, inject more original commentary/thesis, increase structural+visual variation, re-apply after ~30 days.

## Uncertainty flags
1. Per-video watch-hour estimates are illustrative — real numbers hinge entirely on avg view duration (currently weak).
2. YPP human review can take **several weeks after** thresholds are met → 8 weeks gets us *eligible + applied*, not necessarily *approved-and-paying* by then. Budget for review lag.
3. AI-enforcement is tightening through 2026 (auto-labeling live since May 2026) — keep disclosure deliberate and variation high.
