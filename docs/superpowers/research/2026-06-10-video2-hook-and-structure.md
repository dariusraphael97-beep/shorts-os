## Structure (8 chapters, ~8:30) — mini-stories, each ending on a cliffhanger-out

Every chapter is a self-contained story whose **last line opens the next loop** (no flat endings); beats connect with **but / so / therefore** (never "and then"); the villain is always "the myth / the forum consensus / the $20k quote." Every spec is paired with a stakes line, a dollar figure, or a comparison.
